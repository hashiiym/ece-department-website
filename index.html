About the department

The Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering offers a B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering, which has been affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU) since 2015. In 2024, the department expanded its academic framework by introducing an M.Tech programme in Internet of Things (IoT).

Message from the Head of Department

The Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering is committed to building strong academic foundations while encouraging practical design, innovation, and student growth.

Our focus is to create engineers who are technically confident, socially responsible, and ready to contribute across industry, research, and higher studies.

The department continues to strengthen its learning environment through committed faculty support, laboratory experience, and student participation in events, research, and project activity.

Prof. Anwar Hussain M

Mission

"To mould the youth as excellent engineers in Electronics and Communication for the betterment of the society"

Vision

  • To impart theoretical foundations in Electronics and Communication Engineering and instill practical expertise in the related domains and modern tool usage.
  • To inculcate professional and ethical responsibility for teamwork.
  • To enhance communication skills.
